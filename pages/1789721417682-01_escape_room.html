Entrance

Welcome, Ghost Hunters!

It is Halloween night. You and your friends want to visit the old Pumpkin Manor. But when you walk in... the door closes behind you! 👻

A little voice laughs: "You cannot leave, unless you find my secret word!"

You must solve 5 puzzles. Every puzzle gives you one letter. Put all letters together to find the secret word and open the door.

You have about 45 minutes. Good luck! 🦇
Room 1 · The Hall

The Riddle of the Hall

You step into a dark hall. On the wall, you see a riddle written in glowing paint.
"I have no skin, no bones, no face,
yet people say they saw my trace.
I drift through walls without a sound,
at midnight hours I'm often found.
Some people fear me, some feel cold,
but no one ever sees me grow old.
What am I?"

Write your answer in capital letters below.

Hints unlock after 5 minutes of puzzling.
This creature cannot be touched. It has no real body — but people believe they can still see it at night.
The word rhymes with "most" and "toast". It is a spirit that haunts old houses.
Room 2 · The Living Room

The Mixed-Up Letters

On an old table, you find a piece of paper with mixed-up letters. Maybe it is a Halloween word?
U D N A T E H

Some old houses have a special word to describe them: something scary lives inside, and strange noises come from the walls. Unscramble the letters above to find this Halloween word (an adjective, not "house" itself). Write your answer in capital letters.

Hints unlock after 5 minutes of puzzling.
This word is an adjective (a describing word). It often appears right before the word "house" in scary stories.
The word has 7 letters. It rhymes with "wanted" and starts with the same letter as "Halloween".
Room 3 · The Kitchen

The Witch's Cauldron

In the kitchen, a big black cauldron is bubbling. A recipe card lies next to it, but it is a math problem!
There are 6 tables in the kitchen.
Each table holds 3 pumpkins.
During the night, 5 pumpkins roll under the tables and disappear.
In the morning, the cook finds 2 of the lost pumpkins again.

How many pumpkins are on the tables now?

Now use the alphabet code to find your letter:
A=1, B=2, C=3 ... Z=26.
Your number is the answer to the math problem. Find the matching letter and type it below.

Hints unlock after 5 minutes of puzzling.
Solve it step by step, in order: first multiply the tables by the pumpkins per table, then subtract, then add. Don't skip a step!
Your final number is between 10 and 20. Count carefully through the alphabet from A to find the matching letter.
Room 4 · The Attic

The Hidden Creature

Something is hiding in this letter grid... a small, scary creature that likes dark corners!

A Halloween word is hidden somewhere in the grid. It could run left to right, top to bottom, or even along a diagonal line.
Find it and write the word below in capital letters.

Hints unlock after 5 minutes of puzzling.
This creature makes webs in dark, quiet corners of old houses. The word does not run straight across one row and not straight down one column — look for a diagonal line.
The first letter of the hidden word is close to the top-left corner of the grid, in the second row.
Room 5 · The Basement

The Secret Code

In the dark basement, you find a note written in a secret code. Every letter was changed to a different letter, always using the same rule.
Here is one example from the same code:
the letter E in the code means B in normal writing.

Now use this example to discover the rule. Then decode the secret word below:
W U H D W

Write the real word in capital letters below.

Hints unlock after 5 minutes of puzzling.
Look carefully at the example: E → B. How many steps backward in the alphabet is that? Use the same number of steps, but backward, to decode each letter of the secret word.
Every letter in the code was moved 3 steps forward in the alphabet. To read the real word, move each letter 3 steps back.
