Station 1: The Silent Place

I do not move, but I mark a place. I show a name, but not a face. You find me still, beneath the trees, Where flowers rest and no one sees. What am I?

You often find many of these together, in a quiet place with a fence around it.

People visit this place to remember someone who has died. The word starts with the letter G.

Station 2: The Secret Code

Ghosts don't only haunt houses — they also love secret codes! Someone scratched this strange message into an old wall:

K D W

Every letter in the message has been shifted forward by the same number of steps in the alphabet (for example, a shift of 1 turns A into B). Find the correct shift, then shift the letters backward to decode the hidden word. It is something a witch wears.

Try shifting each letter backward. Test a shift of 1 step, then 2, then 3, and check which one makes a real word.

The shift is 3 steps back: K→H, D→A, W→T. It is a pointy piece of clothing worn on the head.

Station 3: Three Costumes

Mia, Noah and Lily are dressed up for Halloween. Each of them wears a different costume: Witch, Owl or Bat. Read the clues carefully:

1. Mia does not wear the Bat costume.
2. Noah does not wear the Witch costume.
3. Lily's costume can fly through the night sky.
4. Noah is afraid of bats, so he did not choose that costume for himself.

What costume is Noah wearing?

Clues 2 and 3 tell you who can NOT be the Witch. Only one person is left — that person is the Witch.

Since Mia is the Witch, only Owl and Bat are left for Noah and Lily. Clue 4 tells you which one is Noah's.

Station 4: Number Ghosts

Solve each calculation below. Then change every number into a letter using this rule: A=1, B=2, C=3 ... Z=26. Write the letters in order to find a creepy, crawly word.

1) 4 × 5 − 1 = ___

2) 20 − 4 = ___

3) 3 × 3 = ___

4) 2 + 2 = ___

5) 10 ÷ 2 = ___

6) 6 × 3 = ___

The numbers are: 19, 16, 9, 4, 5, 18. Now match each number to its letter of the alphabet.

This creature has eight legs and spins webs.

Station 5: Jumbled Words

Children shout a famous phrase at every door on Halloween night. Unscramble the letters below to find the first word of that phrase (the second word means "a sweet treat").

R I K T C

This word rhymes with "brick" and "stick".

The full phrase is: "___ or treat!"
